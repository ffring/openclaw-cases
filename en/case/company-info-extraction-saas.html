What Is an Agentic Workflow and How Does It Work?

Agentic workflows are the next evolution of automation. Unlike traditional scripts or macros that follow a rigid set of instructions, agentic AI agents can make decisions, adapt in real time, and collaborate with other agents to achieve business goals. Think of it as replacing your manual assembly line with a team of smart digital assistants, each handling a specific part of the process—autonomously and without human intervention.

> "Agentic AI workflows are self-directed AI agents that perceive environments, make decisions, and act without human intervention."

In the context of company info extraction, this means:

  • One AI agent crawls target websites automatically
  • Another extracts relevant business details (industry, size, location, contact info)
  • A third updates your CRM in real time
  • All agents work together to deliver structured, ready-to-use data

    • This approach is not only faster but also reduces errors and ensures your team always works with the latest company information.
