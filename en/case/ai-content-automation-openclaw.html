    How Does OpenClaw Work in Practice?

    OpenClaw lets you configure multiple AI agents, each with a specific task. In Prajwal’s setup, five agents work together as a virtual team:

  • Agent 1: Scrapes trending content from Twitter and YouTube
  • Agent 2: Analyzes engagement and ranks content
  • Agent 3: Generates new content based on the hottest topics
  • Agent 4: Schedules posts for optimal times
  • Agent 5: Monitors performance and adjusts strategy

    • This workflow runs continuously, with agents handing off tasks just like a human team. The result? A non-stop content engine that adapts to what’s working and what’s not.

    Benefits of this approach:

  • 24/7 operation—no breaks, no holidays
  • Data-driven decisions on what to post
  • Minimal human oversight required
