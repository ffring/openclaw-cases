How Does Decagon AI Concierge Actually Work?

Decagon positions itself as the "AI concierge for every customer." But what does that mean in practice? At its core, Decagon is a platform that lets businesses build, optimize, and scale AI agents that interact with customers across voice, chat, and email. These agents don't just answer FAQs—they handle complex workflows, remember past conversations, and can even influence sales.

The magic happens through Agent Operating Procedures (AOPs). Instead of writing code or dealing with complicated settings, you define how the AI should behave using natural language. This means business teams—without deep technical skills—can quickly update or create new workflows. No more waiting for engineering sprints or vendor support tickets.

Key features include:

  • Omnichannel coverage: one AI agent works across chat, voice, and email
  • Natural language workflow design (AOPs)
  • Robust testing and analytics to track and improve performance
  • Customization to fit your brand’s tone and values
