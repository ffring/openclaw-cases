    What Makes Fin’s Technology Different?

    The secret sauce behind Fin is its multi-step AI Engine. Here’s how it breaks down complex queries:

    1. Refines the Query: Fin cleans up and clarifies incoming requests, so the AI understands what the customer really wants. 2. Retrieves Relevant Content: Using a proprietary retrieval model, Fin searches all your company’s knowledge sources and finds the most relevant information. 3. Reranks for Precision: The system scores and prioritizes the best answers. 4. Generates a Response: Fin creates a direct, policy-compliant answer—or decides to escalate to a human if needed. 5. Validates Accuracy: Every answer is checked for accuracy and safety before being sent. 6. Engine Optimization: Integrated tools constantly fine-tune performance for better results.

    This robust process ensures that Fin doesn’t just give generic answers—it delivers precise, reliable support tailored to your business.
