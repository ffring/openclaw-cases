What Are AI Agent Platforms and How Do They Work?

AI agent platforms are not just smarter chatbots. Unlike traditional bots that only answer questions, these systems actively execute tasks across your business tools. Instead of a request-response loop, AI agents connect to real apps via APIs and run multi-step workflows—autonomously.

For example, a chatbot might draft a message for Slack. An AI agent, on the other hand, can:

  • Read your Stripe dashboard
  • Identify failed payments
  • Look up the customer in HubSpot
  • Alert the account manager in Slack—all automatically and on schedule

    • > "A chatbot can draft a Slack message. An AI agent can handle the entire failed payment workflow without human intervention."

    This leap means you’re not just saving clicks—you’re freeing up real hours. According to the source, by 2028, 33% of enterprise software will include agentic AI, up from less than 1% in 2024 (Gartner).
