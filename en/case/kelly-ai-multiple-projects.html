How Does KellyClaudeAI Manage Multiple Projects at Once?

The core innovation here is the combination of KellyClaudeAI—a smart AI agent—and the OpenClaw Deck, a user interface designed for multitasking. Each column in the OpenClaw Deck represents a unique session, or thread, of Kelly. Within each column, Kelly can spawn subagents, allowing for even more granular task management.

This means you can have one instance of Kelly working on, say, submitting your app to the App Store, while another is deep-diving into your company’s strategy. Switching between these projects is as simple as hitting a hotkey. No more opening dozens of tabs or losing your train of thought.

  • Multiple project threads, each with its own focus
  • Instant hotkey switching between sessions
  • Subagents for handling specific subtasks

    How Can You Implement This in Your Business?

    Getting started with KellyClaudeAI and OpenClaw Deck is about rethinking how you structure your work. Instead of assigning individual tasks, you set up project sessions and let the AI handle the details. The key is to clearly define the scope of each session and use subagents for complex or multi-step tasks.

    Steps to get started:

  • Identify the main projects you want to run in parallel
  • Set up a session (column) for each in the OpenClaw Deck
  • Assign subtasks to subagents as needed
  • Use hotkeys to switch focus and monitor progress

    • This approach works best for entrepreneurs and teams who need to juggle multiple priorities without losing momentum.

    What Are the Limitations and Challenges?

    While the setup is powerful, it’s not without challenges. The main limitation is the AI’s ability to understand complex, nuanced tasks. You’ll still need to provide clear instructions and check in on progress. There’s also a learning curve as you adapt to managing projects through an AI interface instead of traditional tools.

    Potential challenges:

  • Defining clear project scopes for the AI
  • Monitoring subagent performance
  • Adapting your workflow to a new interface

    • Overall, the benefits outweigh the drawbacks for most entrepreneurs, especially those managing multiple projects at once.
