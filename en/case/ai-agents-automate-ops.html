How Did the Automation Start? Connecting Voice Journals to AI Ops

The journey began with a simple but powerful idea: what if your daily voice journals could feed directly into your business workflows? Using Obsidian as a vault for voice notes, the entrepreneur set up an AI agent (via OpenClaw) to tap into these recordings. Now, instead of letting ideas sit idle, every voice memo could trigger next steps automatically.

  • Obsidian stores all voice journals securely
  • OpenClaw connects to Obsidian and listens for new entries
  • Each new voice note becomes actionable data

    This first step eliminated the gap between brainstorming and execution. No more forgotten ideas. The AI agent kept the business moving forward without manual intervention.

    How Did the Workflow Expand? Integrating ClickUp for Task Management

    Once the AI agent could process voice journals, the next challenge was: how do you turn those insights into real tasks? The answer was ClickUp—a popular project management tool. By wiring the ClickUp API into the AI agent’s workflow, the entrepreneur ensured that actionable items didn’t just sit in notes—they became tracked, assigned tasks.

    Here’s how it worked:

  • AI agent extracts tasks and deadlines from voice notes or documents
  • ClickUp API automatically creates tasks and assigns them to the right team member
  • No manual data entry, no lost action items

    • This integration turned a messy, manual process into a streamlined, automated pipeline. The result? Faster project kickoffs and fewer dropped balls.

    How Was Client Input Automated? Extracting Deliverables from Proposals

    Perhaps the most impressive part of this automation was feeding a client proposal PDF directly into the AI agent. Instead of reading through the document and manually entering each deliverable, the entrepreneur let the AI do the heavy lifting. The agent auto-extracted 14 deliverables—each with its own deadline—and pushed them straight into ClickUp.

  • Client sends a proposal PDF
  • AI agent scans and parses the document
  • All deliverables and deadlines are extracted and structured
  • Tasks are created and assigned instantly

    • > "Fed it a client proposal PDF → Auto-extracted 14 deliverables with deadlines → Assigned all tasks to my team lead…"

    This step alone can save hours per client, especially for agencies or consultancies juggling multiple projects.

    How Did the Team Stay Aligned? Building a Tracking System in Discord

    Automation isn’t just about creating tasks—it’s about keeping everyone in the loop. To solve this, the entrepreneur built a simple yet effective client tracking system in Discord. Now, any updates or new tasks could be surfaced directly in the team’s chat platform, ensuring nothing slips through the cracks.

  • Discord serves as the central hub for notifications and updates
  • AI agent posts task assignments and progress automatically
  • Team lead and members get real-time visibility

    • This approach reduces email overload and keeps the team focused on what matters: delivering for clients.
