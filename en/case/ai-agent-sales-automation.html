How Does OpenClaw Automate Outbound Sales?

Traditional outbound sales is a slog. You write cold emails, research leads, book calls, and take notes—all manually. Johann’s setup with OpenClaw flips this on its head. Here’s his workflow:

  • OpenClaw sends cold emails to leads automatically every night
  • When a lead books a call, OpenClaw researches the lead before the meeting
  • After the call, Fathom records and transcribes the conversation
  • Fathom sends the transcript back to OpenClaw for analysis

    • This means the only human task is showing up and having the call. Everything else—from outreach to research to follow-up—is handled by AI.

    > “I automated my sales cycle with OpenClaw. Now I focus on high-impact calls, not busywork.”

    • How Does the AI Research Leads Before Calls?

    One of the most powerful features in Johann’s workflow is pre-call research. Here’s how it works:

    When a lead books a call, OpenClaw automatically gathers data—company info, recent news, social profiles, and more. By the time you join the call, you have a dossier on your prospect.

    This lets you:

  • Personalize your pitch
  • Reference recent company events
  • Ask smarter questions

    • > “OpenClaw does deep research on the lead before the call. I go in fully prepared, every time.”

    How Does Fathom Enhance the Workflow?

    Fathom is a meeting assistant that records and transcribes calls. In Johann’s setup, it works hand-in-hand with OpenClaw:

  • During the call, Fathom records and transcribes everything
  • After the call, the transcript is sent to OpenClaw automatically
  • OpenClaw can then analyze the transcript for action items, objections, and next steps

    • This means you never miss a detail. Follow-ups are timely and laser-focused.

    The Integration Flow:

  • Record: Every call is captured, no manual notes
  • Transcribe: Instant, searchable transcript
  • Analyze: AI pulls out key insights
