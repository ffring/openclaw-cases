Why ChatGPT Agent Mode Matters for Lead Generation Now

The world of B2B sales and marketing is changing fast. If you're an entrepreneur or business owner in Russia or the CIS, you know how time-consuming it is to find new clients. Manual research, endless spreadsheets, and hours spent on Google — all just to build a list of potential leads. But what if you could automate most of this process, freeing up your time to focus on closing deals?

AI agents like ChatGPT's Agent Mode are opening new doors for business automation. No longer just a chatbot, ChatGPT can now act as a research assistant, data analyst, and even a junior sales rep. In this article, you'll learn exactly how one user leveraged Agent Mode to find and format high-quality leads — and how you can apply the same tactics in your business.

How Does ChatGPT Agent Mode Work for Lead Generation?

ChatGPT's Agent Mode isn't just about answering questions. It's about giving the AI a task — like "find me 10 more clients just like this one" — and letting it handle the heavy lifting. In the real-world case shared on Reddit, the user started by providing the AI with a website URL of an ideal client. They also shared articles that explained the company's strengths and specialties.

From there, the user asked ChatGPT Agent Mode to find 10, then 20, similar companies. The agent researched, compiled, and presented the results in a structured format. This structure was crucial: it allowed for easy upload into tools like Clay or Apollo for further enrichment and outreach. The result? Hours of manual work condensed into minutes.

> "Once you have it doing that, you’ll find more and more tasks it can do (and save you a ton of time)."

  • No more manual Googling for similar companies
  • Automated formatting for lead enrichment platforms
  • Scalable process: repeat as needed for new markets or niches

    • What Are the Steps to Set Up ChatGPT Agent Mode for Lead Research?

    Getting started is simpler than you might think. First, identify your "ideal customer" — this could be an existing client or a company profile you want to target. Gather as much context as possible: website, case studies, articles, or press releases. The more you feed the agent, the better its results.

    Next, instruct ChatGPT Agent Mode clearly. For example: "Find 10 companies similar to [Client X], focusing on [industry/vertical], and present the results in a table with columns for company name, website, and focus area." The agent will use its knowledge and web skills to research and compile the list.

    Once you receive the output, you can:

  • Review and edit the results for accuracy
  • Upload the formatted data to lead enrichment tools like Clay or Apollo
  • Repeat the process with new criteria to expand your outreach

    • How Do You Integrate ChatGPT Results with Clay or Apollo?

    Finding leads is just the first step. To turn research into actionable sales opportunities, you need to enrich and contact those leads. Clay and Apollo are popular platforms for this, letting you add email addresses, LinkedIn profiles, and other key data.

    The Reddit user specifically asked ChatGPT to format its output in a "cab format" (custom structured format) suitable for upload. This saves time and reduces errors compared to manual copy-pasting. With a well-structured CSV or table, you can:

  • Bulk upload leads to Clay or Apollo
  • Automatically enrich data with contact info
  • Launch email campaigns or outreach sequences

    • > "I even ask it to present to me in a cab format that I can upload to Clay or Apollo for enrichment — and it does that!"

    What Tasks Can ChatGPT Agent Mode Automate Beyond Lead Generation?

    Once you've set up Agent Mode for one task, it's easy to expand its use. The Reddit user noted that after automating lead research, they found "more and more tasks it can do."

    Some examples include:

  • Competitor analysis: Find and summarize competitors' strengths
  • Content research: Identify trending topics or gaps in your industry
  • Data formatting: Clean and standardize lists for CRM upload

    • This flexibility means you can continually delegate repetitive, research-heavy tasks to the AI, freeing your team for higher-value work.

    What Are the Benefits and Limitations of This Approach?

    The main benefit is obvious: time saved. Manual lead research can take hours per week, especially if you target multiple industries. With ChatGPT Agent Mode, you get:

  • Faster lead list creation
  • Consistent formatting
  • Less human error

    • However, there are limitations. The AI's research is only as good as the data and instructions you provide. It may miss niche companies or misunderstand complex criteria. Human review is still important for final quality control.

    How Can Entrepreneurs in Russia/CIS Start Using AI Agents for Growth?

    If you're ready to try this in your business, start small. Pick one client profile and use ChatGPT Agent Mode to build a list of similar companies. Test the process with a few leads, upload to your favorite enrichment tool, and track the results.

    Remember:

  • Be specific with instructions
  • Review AI-generated lists for accuracy
  • Use the time saved to focus on closing deals or expanding your market

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    How do I access ChatGPT Agent Mode? ChatGPT Agent Mode is available in certain versions of ChatGPT. Check your OpenAI account or platform settings to enable it.

    Do I need to know programming to use Agent Mode? No programming is required. Clear instructions and context are enough to get started.

    Can I use these tools in Russian or for Russian companies? Yes, you can provide instructions and context in Russian, and focus your research on Russian or CIS markets.

    What if the AI makes a mistake or misses a good lead? Always review the results before uploading to your CRM or outreach tool. Human oversight ensures quality.

    How much time can this save me? While results vary, users report saving several hours per week on manual research and formatting.
