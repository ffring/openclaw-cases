How Does Claude Code Automate App Store Submissions?

Claude Code, a powerful AI framework, has been successfully integrated with Apple’s App Store Connect via a native macOS app called Blitz. This setup allows Claude Code—or any compatible client—to take full control over the app submission and review process. Tasks that used to require manual attention, like entering metadata, uploading builds, and responding to reviewer feedback, are now handled automatically.

  • Metadata entry (name, description, screenshots)
  • Build uploads and versioning
  • Automated responses to review feedback

    • > "The significant implication here is the automation of tedious and error-prone tasks traditionally performed manually, ranging from metadata entry to managing build uploads and responding to review feedback."

    This isn’t just a theoretical demo. Developers have already used this pipeline to submit real apps, drastically reducing the manual effort and time involved. The result: faster releases, fewer mistakes, and a more reliable deployment process.

    What Is Ultraplan and Why Is Human Oversight Important?

    Ultraplan, introduced in Claude Code v2.1.92, brings a new level of transparency and control to AI-driven workflows. Instead of letting an AI agent execute tasks blindly, Ultraplan separates the planning phase from execution. You can draft complex workflows in the cloud, review each step in a web browser (with inline comments), and only then approve execution—either remotely or via the command line.

    This is crucial for businesses that need accountability and reliability. Mistakes in app submissions can mean costly delays or even rejections from Apple. With Ultraplan, you can:

  • Review every action before it happens
  • Collaborate with your team on workflow plans
  • Ensure compliance and reduce risk

    • > "Ultraplan addresses a common challenge in agentic AI systems: the need for transparent planning and human oversight."

    For entrepreneurs and business owners, this means you can trust AI agents to handle the grunt work—without losing visibility or control over critical business processes.

    What Are the Risks and Limitations?

    While automation brings speed and efficiency, it also introduces new risks. If an AI agent makes a mistake—such as submitting the wrong metadata or missing a critical compliance step—it could lead to app rejection or reputational damage. That’s why features like Ultraplan’s review interface are so important.

  • Human-in-the-loop validation is key
  • Transparent workflows reduce surprises
  • Still requires some technical setup and oversight

    • It’s also worth noting that while Claude Code and Blitz reduce manual effort, they don’t eliminate the need for strategic decision-making. You still need to decide what to ship, when, and how to position your app in the market.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Q: Do I need to be a developer to use Claude Code for app submissions? A: Some technical setup is required, but the tools are becoming more user-friendly. Non-developers may need initial help but can benefit from the automation once it’s running.

    Q: Is my data safe when using AI agents for App Store submissions? A: With Ultraplan’s human-in-the-loop review, you control what actions the AI takes. Always review workflows and use secure connections to protect your data.

    Q: Can automation handle App Store review feedback automatically? A: Yes, Claude Code can generate and submit responses to reviewer comments, saving time and reducing manual effort.

    Q: What if something goes wrong during automated submission? A: Ultraplan allows you to review and approve every step, reducing risk. If issues arise, you can intervene before final submission.

    Q: How quickly can I expect results after setup? A: Some users reported launching multiple apps in weeks, thanks to automation. Your timeline will depend on your specific workflow and app complexity.
