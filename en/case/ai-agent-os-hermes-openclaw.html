How Does an AI Agent Operating System Work?

An AI Agent Operating System (AI Agent OS) is not just another app—it's an orchestrator. Think of it as the command center that brings multiple AI agents together, allowing them to collaborate and automate complex tasks. In Julian Goldie's case, the AI Agent OS connects directly to Hermes and OpenClaw, two powerful AI tools.

  • Hermes acts as the backbone, managing the communication and workflow between agents.
  • OpenClaw specializes in generating creative assets like videos, images, and speech.

    • This integration means that instead of jumping between different tools or platforms, everything happens in one place. For example, you could:

  • Generate a script with one agent
  • Turn that script into a narrated video with another
  • Create supporting images automatically
  • Publish the final content—all within the same system

    • > "All my AI agents work together inside a single engine that automates everything for me."

    The result? Seamless, end-to-end automation for content creation, tailored to your business needs.

    How Are Hermes and OpenClaw Used Together?

    Hermes and OpenClaw complement each other perfectly in this setup. Hermes is the control layer: it manages the flow of tasks, decides which agent does what, and ensures everything happens in the right order. OpenClaw is the creative powerhouse, generating the actual media assets.

    Here's how a typical workflow might look:

  • Hermes receives a content brief (e.g., "Create a video about our new service")
  • Hermes assigns tasks to different agents: script writing, image generation, voice synthesis
  • OpenClaw generates videos and images based on the script
  • Hermes coordinates the assembly of all assets into a final product

    • This approach means you can scale your content efforts without scaling your team. You can even automate responses to market trends, creating timely content as soon as new opportunities arise.

  • Key benefits:
    • - Single dashboard for all tasks - Automated hand-offs between agents - Customizable workflows for different content types
