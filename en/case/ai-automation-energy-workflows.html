What Problems Do Legacy Workflows Cause in Energy Companies?

Traditional workflows in energy companies depend heavily on manual data entry, paper logbooks, and siloed approval systems. This creates several pain points:

  • Slow decision-making due to manual sign-offs
  • High risk of errors and compliance breaches
  • Difficulty scaling or adapting to regulatory changes

    • For companies like Dresser Natural Gas Solutions, these issues meant wasted hours, frustrated staff, and missed opportunities for innovation. According to FlowForma, energy sector firms using AI-powered automation have seen significant improvements in efficiency and cost savings.

    > "AI-powered automation replaces code-heavy legacy workflows with auto-assigning records, unifying process changes and approvals in one place."

    When approvals, records, and compliance tracking are unified in a single automated platform, the whole business moves faster. Teams can focus on analysis and strategy, not paperwork.
