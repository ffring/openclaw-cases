    How Does Zendesk Ensure Quality and Control?

    Automation is only valuable if quality doesn’t slip. Zendesk addresses this with automatic resolution validation and built-in QA scoring for every AI agent interaction. This means you stay in control—no more worrying about bots going rogue or delivering poor answers.

    The Zendesk Resolution Platform ties together:

  • Tickets, live chat, voice, and knowledge bases
  • Quality assurance dashboards
  • Analytics for tracking performance and identifying areas for improvement

    • With transparent AI reasoning and the ability to fine-tune responses, you can continuously improve both customer experience and operational efficiency. If an AI agent can’t resolve an issue, it seamlessly escalates to a human agent—ensuring no customer is left behind.
