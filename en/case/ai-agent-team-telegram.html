INTRO

Imagine running your business from your phone while a team of AI agents works around the clock. No missed deadlines, no repetitive tasks, no burnout. For entrepreneurs and business owners in Russia and the CIS, this isn’t a distant dream—it’s happening right now, thanks to AI automation via platforms like Telegram.

Managing a business, especially one with a global or digital footprint, means juggling endless research, content, updates, and reviews. Most founders feel the pain: too much to do, not enough time, and constant pressure to stay ahead. What if you could delegate most of this to a tireless, always-on team that learns your preferences and improves itself?

In this article, you’ll discover how Shubham Saboo built a real AI agent team that runs 24/7, manages a massive open-source project, and keeps him ahead—using only Telegram as the interface. We’ll break down the tools, the workflow, and the results, so you can see if this revolution fits your business.

How Does an AI Agent Team Work in Practice?

Shubham Saboo’s setup isn’t a tech demo or a weekend experiment. It’s a robust workflow where six AI agents manage critical business tasks, day and night. The agents aren’t just chatbots—they proactively handle research, draft content, review code, and even curate a newsletter, all while keeping track of past decisions and preferences.

The core of this system is automation. Agents are scheduled to run on specific intervals (using Cron jobs and heartbeats), so they don’t just wait for commands—they act on their own, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks. This proactive approach means the business keeps moving, even when the owner is offline or asleep.

> "Six AI agents run my entire life while I sleep. Not a demo. Not a weekend project. A real team that works 24/7 making sure I’m never behind."

Key features include:

  • Automated schedules for routine tasks
  • Contextual memory of preferences, audience, and performance
  • Self-review and squad-review cycles for continuous improvement
  • Final decision-making remains with the human owner

    • What Makes This AI Setup Different from Simple Bots?

    Unlike basic bots that just respond to commands, Saboo’s agents are designed to work like a real team. They remember context—your preferences, your audience, what’s worked in the past—and use that to make smarter decisions. Each agent has a specialized role, and their work is reviewed regularly, both by themselves and by a lead agent (Monica), ensuring quality and consistency.

    This isn’t just automation; it’s intelligent delegation. The agents:

  • Review their own performance monthly
  • Undergo bi-weekly squad reviews
  • Escalate important decisions to the owner

    • This means you get the benefits of a real team—proactivity, learning, and accountability—without the overhead of hiring and managing people.

    > "Monica reviews the entire squad bi-weekly and grades them. They do the work, but I still make the final decision."

    How Is Telegram Used as the Control Center?

    One of the most striking aspects of Saboo’s workflow is the use of Telegram as the main interface. No complex dashboards, no need to log in to multiple tools. Everything flows through a familiar messenger app.

    From Telegram, Saboo can:

  • Receive updates and daily signals from agents
  • Review and approve (or reject) their work
  • Make final decisions on escalated issues
  • Operate like a CEO on the go

    • This approach drastically lowers the barrier to entry. If you can use Telegram, you can control your AI team. It’s especially relevant for business owners in Russia and the CIS, where Telegram is a dominant communication tool.

    How Can This Approach Scale to Real Business Needs?

    Saboo’s AI team manages the open-source "Awesome LLM Apps" repository, which boasts over 115,000 GitHub stars. That’s not a side project—it’s a massive, high-visibility operation. The agents handle:

  • Researching new tools and trends
  • Drafting and updating content
  • Reviewing code changes
  • Sending proactive daily signals

    • All this is done with minimal manual intervention. The owner steps in only for final approvals or major decisions. This setup shows that AI agents can take on real, high-stakes business tasks—not just toy problems.

    > "These agents manage the open-source Awesome LLM Apps repo with 115k+ GitHub stars and help me stay up-to-date with pro-active daily signals."

    What Results Can You Expect from an AI Agent Team?

    The most obvious benefit is time saved. By automating research, content drafting, and review cycles, Saboo can focus on strategic decisions instead of daily grind. The AI agents keep everything moving, ensuring nothing is forgotten or delayed.

    Other key outcomes include:

  • Consistent quality thanks to self-improving review loops
  • Better decision-making with contextual memory
  • The freedom to operate as a “CEO” from anywhere, via phone

    • For entrepreneurs, this means more time for growth, less stress, and the ability to scale operations without scaling headcount.

    What Are the Key Tools Used in This AI Workflow?

    Saboo’s system is built on two main platforms:

  • OpenClaw: Where the AI agent team started. Handles the core automation and scheduling.
  • Hermes: The next evolution, enhancing the team’s capabilities and workflows.

    • Both are integrated through Telegram, making the entire process seamless. The agents’ ability to remember context and improve over time is what sets this stack apart from simple automation scripts.

    > "OpenClaw is where the squad started. Hermes is where it’s evolving. YES, I use both of THEM just via Telegram."

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    How much technical skill do I need to set this up?

    If you can use Telegram, you can manage your AI agent team. The setup may require initial configuration, but day-to-day use is as simple as chatting in a messenger app.

    Will AI agents replace my employees?

    AI agents are best for automating repetitive, routine tasks. They free up your team to focus on strategy, creativity, and complex decisions—not replace them entirely.

    How secure is it to manage business tasks via Telegram?

    Telegram is widely used for business in Russia/CIS and offers strong privacy features. Still, always follow best practices for account and data security.

    Can I customize what the AI agents do?

    Yes. The agents can be set up to handle different roles—research, content, code review, and more—based on your business needs.

    What if the AI makes a mistake?

    The system is designed with escalation in mind. AI agents handle routine work, but you make all final decisions. Regular reviews ensure quality and catch errors early.

With platforms like OpenClaw and Hermes, and a familiar interface like Telegram, business owners in Russia and the CIS can harness 24/7 automation today.

    If you’re tired of juggling endless tasks and want to see what real AI delegation looks like, start exploring AI agent platforms. You could be running your business like a CEO—from your phone—sooner than you think.
