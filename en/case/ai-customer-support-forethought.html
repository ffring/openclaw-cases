How Do Forethought’s AI Agents Work in Customer Support?

Forethought’s platform is built around “fully agentic” AI agents. Unlike basic chatbots that just answer scripted questions, these agents can reason, decide, and act based on your business policies. They learn from your historical support tickets and help center content, so they get smarter over time and deliver personalized answers from day one.

> “Forethought’s AI agents don’t just understand intent—they reason, decide, and take action using your business policies to deliver end-to-end AI customer support.”

The system supports multiple channels: chat, email, voice, and more. It’s a multi-agent setup—meaning different AI agents can collaborate to classify, solve, and escalate tickets as needed. This allows you to:

  • Automatically classify and route tickets to the right human agent
  • Instantly resolve common customer issues with AI
  • Surface real-time insights and recommendations to human agents

    • Forethought integrates with leading helpdesk platforms, so you don’t need to rebuild your support stack. The result? Faster, smarter, and more efficient customer service.

    What Does a Multi-Agent AI System Look Like in Practice?

    Forethought’s multi-agent system sets it apart from simple chatbots. Here’s how it works in a real support workflow:

    1. Ticket arrives: AI classifies and tags the ticket, adding context and priority. 2. AI triage: If it’s a common issue, the AI can resolve it instantly—no human needed. 3. Routing: If human input is needed, the AI routes the ticket to the best-qualified agent. 4. Agent assist: Copilot provides relevant info and suggested responses in real time. 5. Insights: The system analyzes all interactions, surfacing knowledge gaps and recommending new help articles.

    This end-to-end approach means fewer dropped tickets, faster resolutions, and continuous improvement.
