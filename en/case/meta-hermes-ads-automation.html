What Is Meta’s New Ads CLI and Why Should You Care?

Meta’s new Ads CLI is an official command line tool for managing Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns. Before this, most businesses had to rely on browser dashboards or third-party plugins, which meant lots of clicking, waiting, and room for mistakes. Now, with the CLI, you get direct, authorized access to your ad accounts—no more jumping between tabs or dealing with clunky interfaces.

  • The CLI allows you to:
    • - Create, edit, or pause campaigns instantly - Pull real-time analytics without logging in - Automate repetitive tasks via scripts or AI agents

    > "What makes this announcement so interesting is that it gives AI tools direct, authorized access to help manage your Meta Ads account through natural [language]," said Bryan Cano, a digital marketing expert.

    For business owners, this means less time spent on manual adjustments and more time for high-level decision making. If you already use AI chatbots or assistants, this CLI is the missing link to put your ad management on autopilot.

    How Does Hermes AI Integrate with Meta Ads CLI?

    Hermes is an AI agent designed to handle routine business tasks. With the new skill built by Mason James, Hermes can now connect directly to Meta’s Ads CLI. This integration lets Hermes:

  • Understand your campaign goals via natural language
  • Execute changes in your Meta Ads account automatically
  • Fetch reports or analytics on demand

    • Imagine telling Hermes, “Pause all ads with high cost-per-click,” and having it done in seconds—no human needed. The integration is built on the open-source "OpenClaw" version, ensuring transparency and flexibility for businesses of any size.

    > "I wanted Hermes to leverage these tools, so I built a skill for it," Mason James shared after the launch.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    How safe is it to let AI manage my Meta Ads account?

    Hermes uses authorized, secure access via Meta’s official CLI. You control which actions the AI can perform, and you can review all changes. Start with limited permissions and expand as you gain trust.

    Do I need to know coding or hire a developer?

    No. The integration is designed for non-technical users. Setup involves installing the CLI and connecting your account, then giving instructions in plain language.

    Can Hermes AI make creative decisions for my ads?

    Hermes can automate routine management tasks, but creative decisions—like ad copy or targeting—still require human input. Use AI for the grunt work, and keep creative strategy in your hands.

    What if something goes wrong with my campaigns?

    You can set Hermes to notify you of any changes or issues. Always review campaign results regularly and keep backups of your settings.

    Is this tool available for small businesses or only large agencies?

    Both. The open-source nature and simple setup make it accessible for businesses of any size, from solo entrepreneurs to large marketing teams.
