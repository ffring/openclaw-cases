Why AI-Powered Risk Assessments Matter for Your Business Now

Imagine being able to multiply your company’s safety checks by six, without hiring more staff or sacrificing quality. For business owners and managers in Russia and the CIS, the pressure to scale operations while maintaining strict safety standards is real. Manual risk assessments are time-consuming, expensive, and often bottlenecked by expert availability. If you want to grow and stay compliant, you need a smarter way.

That’s where AI-powered agents come in. Cando Rail & Terminals, a major player in rail logistics, faced this exact challenge. By partnering with Fractional AI, they built "Peter the Safety Agent"—an AI system that automates and accelerates risk assessments. The results? What Was the Problem with Traditional Risk Assessments?

For Cando Rail, safety is non-negotiable. But their risk assessment process was a headache. Each assessment meant up to 16 hours of meetings with subject matter experts (SMEs), weeks of back-and-forth on revisions, and endless manual cross-referencing of PDFs. Learning from previous reports was nearly impossible, and scaling up meant even more time and cost.

  • Up to 16 hours of SME meetings per assessment
  • Weeks spent on revisions
  • Heavy manual work, little knowledge transfer

    • > "The manual process couldn’t scale: each assessment required up to 16 hours of SME meetings, weeks of revisions, and heavy PDF cross-referencing, with limited ability to learn from past assessments."

    The pain point was clear: Cando wanted to multiply the number of annual risk assessments at the heart of its safety program, but the process just wouldn’t scale. Human experts needed to remain in control—but the paperwork and logistics were holding them back.

    How Did AI Transform the Risk Assessment Workflow?

    Enter Peter the Safety Agent, a custom-built AI solution using GPT-4.1, GPT-realtime, Recall.ai, LangChain, and Microsoft Teams integration. The workflow was redesigned to keep human experts in the driver’s seat, while automating everything else.

  • Prework automation: The AI generates a first draft of the risk assessment in seconds, before the meeting even starts.
  • In-meeting voice assistant: During SME discussions, the AI transcribes and structures the conversation in real time, updating the risk assessment draft on the fly.
  • Seamless collaboration: All updates and drafts are instantly available to the team, reducing the need for endless revisions.

    • This means safety experts can focus on decision-making, not paperwork. The AI handles the heavy lifting, but humans remain fully responsible for every safety call.

    What Tools and Models Powered the Solution?

    Cando Rail’s solution wasn’t just about plugging in ChatGPT. It combined several advanced tools:

  • GPT-4.1 & GPT-realtime: For natural language understanding, summarization, and drafting reports.
  • Recall.ai: To transcribe and structure live conversations during meetings.
  • LangChain: To orchestrate the workflow and connect different AI modules.
  • Microsoft Teams integration: To fit seamlessly into the company’s existing collaboration tools.

    • Each tool played a unique role. For example, Recall.ai captured every detail of expert discussions, while GPT-4.1 turned those details into clear, structured reports. The integration with Teams meant no new apps or training were needed—AI worked right where the team already collaborated.

    > "Prework automation produces a first draft of the risk assessment in seconds; An in-meeting voice assistant transcribes and structures SME discussions in real time, generating readily updated risk assessment drafts as the conversation unfolds."

    What Are the Practical Steps to Implement AI Risk Assessment?

    If you’re ready to get started, follow these steps:
