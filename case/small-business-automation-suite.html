Complete Small Business Automation Suite with OpenClaw

ClawDock documented a comprehensive automation suite that saves small businesses 15+ hours per week by deploying OpenClaw bots across Telegram, WhatsApp, Slack, and Discord channels. The system handles routine work 24/7 while business owners focus on growth activities.

Key Business Automations

Customer Support & Communication: Automated customer support bot deployed on WhatsApp/Telegram answers 80% of routine queries using OpenClaw's knowledge base skill fed with product documentation. The bot handles FAQ responses, order status checks, and escalates complex issues to human agents.

Appointment & Lead Management: Google Calendar integration through the calendar skill enables customers to book appointments via natural conversation. A separate Telegram bot qualifies leads by asking scoring questions and routes hot prospects directly to sales team Slack channels. Invoice and payment reminders are sent via personalized WhatsApp messages, proving more effective than generic email campaigns.

Content & Inventory Operations: The content creation skill maintains consistent brand voice across social media platforms, drafting posts and suggesting hashtags for scheduling. Inventory monitoring automatically sends proactive stock alerts when items drop below threshold levels, essential for e-commerce businesses managing multiple SKUs.

Internal Process Automation

Employee & Meeting Management: Slack-deployed onboarding bot guides new hires through company policies, tool setup, and training resources at their own pace. Meeting transcripts are automatically processed into structured notes with action items, deadlines, and assignments distributed to relevant Slack channels.

Monitoring & Intelligence: Web scraping skills monitor competitor prices, track search rankings, and deliver weekly SEO reports via Telegram. Email triage summarizes incoming messages, flags urgent items, and drafts responses with daily Telegram digests prioritized by importance.

Technical Implementation

The system achieves <2 minute average response times with 24/7 availability. ClawDock's managed platform provides pre-configured skills and channel integrations, eliminating the manual setup required for self-hosting across multiple messaging platforms.
